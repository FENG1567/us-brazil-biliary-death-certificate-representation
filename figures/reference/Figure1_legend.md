Figure 1 | Cohort construction, parallel released representations and validation boundaries. (a) Raw death records were restricted to the country-specific resident universe and then to analytic age-sex resident records; UCD and country-specific any_common are representation endpoints and are not mutually exclusive. (b) The three released representations are parallel analysis definitions, not an ordered sequence or containment hierarchy. UCD denotes underlying cause. In the United States, any_common is UCD or an entity-axis mention; in Brazil, it is UCD or a saved certificate-line mention. US-driven structural sensitivity uses UCD or a record-axis mention in the United States and equals any_common by definition in Brazil. (c) The matrix separates exact national-source coverage from retained scope boundaries. US CDC WONDER exact checks cover UCD and the record-axis alternative for 2018-2024; Brazil TabNet exact checks cover annual UCD and all-cause resident totals for 2012-2024. The figure does not imply cross-country semantic equivalence, clinical equivalence, causal direction, certificate quality or coding accuracy.
