Figure 2 | Annual released-representation rates and system-specific conditional UCD fractions, 2012-2024. (a,b) Direct WHO18 age-standardized K80-K83 mortality representation rates in the United States and Brazil, respectively, using sex weights of 0.5/0.5. UCD denotes underlying cause. country-specific any_common is UCD or an entity-axis mention in the United States and UCD or a saved certificate-line mention in Brazil; it is therefore not a semantically equivalent cross-country endpoint. Ribbons show 95% Poisson counting log intervals. (c) Country-year P(UCD K80-K83 | country-specific any_common K80-K83) under the same target distribution; ribbons show 95% model-conditional delta intervals scaled by Pearson dispersion. This quantity is not a clinical disease probability, certificate-quality score, coding-accuracy measure or causal estimand. Confidence intervals do not include national-system measurement error.
